🚀 Cloudflare Pages Upload Helper

⚠️ Important: You need to manually upload the files to Cloudflare Pages dashboard. This helper provides the exact steps and file locations.
1 Open Cloudflare Pages Dashboard
Open Dashboard
2 Find Your Project
Look for your existing noamba project and click on it.
3 Go to Deployments
Click on the "Deployments" tab at the top.
4 Upload Assets
Click "Upload assets" or "Deploy from folder" button.
📁 Files to Upload:
Option A (Recommended): Upload the compressed package:
noamba-static-deployment.tar.gz

Option B: Upload the source folder:
src/ folder (drag and drop the entire folder)
5 Deploy
Review the files and click "Deploy" or "Deploy to production".
6 Test Your Deployment
Once deployed, test both modes:

Production Mode:
https://noamba.pages.dev

Staging Mode:
https://noamba.pages.dev?staging=true
(Should show orange [STAGING] badge)
✅ What's Included:
  • Environment detection (staging vs production)
  • Visual staging indicator (orange badge)
  • Console logging for environment
  • URL parameter support (?staging=true)
  • All your MultiGender Hebrew features
🔧 If You Need Help:
The deployment package contains all your files ready to upload. Just follow the steps above and you'll have your staging environment working!
